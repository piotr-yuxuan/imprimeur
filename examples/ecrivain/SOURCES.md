# Sources of the samples

`carnet.org` and `notebook.md` were written to try this skin, and each says so in its first
lines: their prose, recipes and measurements are invented, as are `plume.svg`, `ecriture.webm`
and its poster `ecriture.png`, under the repository's licence.

Each holds one quotation, checked on 3 November 2026:

- `carnet.org`: Nicolas Boileau, *L'Art poétique*, chant I, 1674, as [French
  Wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Boileau_-_%C5%92uvres_po%C3%A9tiques/L%E2%80%99Art_po%C3%A9tique/Chant_I)
  gives it from *Œuvres poétiques*, Imprimerie générale, 1872, vol. 1, p. 203-211.
- `notebook.md`: Alexander Pope, *An Essay on Criticism*, 1711, lines 362 and 363, as
  [Representative Poetry Online](https://rpo.library.utoronto.ca/content/essay-criticism-part-2)
  transcribes London: Lewis, 1711.
