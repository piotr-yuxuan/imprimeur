# Sources of the samples

Both are real texts, each set under an essay's head that says what it adds. No scene here is
invented.

| File | Holds | Source |
|---|---|---|

Everything else in each file says that it is the essay's, by a comment in its source and by its
own words on the page: the table, the code, the diagrams, the notes and the two films, which were
made from the engraving and the plate, and which are under the repository's licence. The sample of
Wells leaves out, and says so, a passage on the novel's colonial police, in the language of its day.

## The check of the quotations

Each sample promises that its author's paragraphs are quoted to the letter. A program checked each
paragraph outside the essay's marks against the witnesses above on 2026-11-03: 45 paragraphs and the
note in French, and 10 in English, all found, with one misprint of the French corrected, "de, Wagner",
which the sample says. The two chapters' sources are not copied here. Each dash of dialogue in the
French is written U+2015, the horizontal bar, as this repository holds no em dash; that is the one
sign that differs from the witness.
