# Sources of the samples

`robida.org` and `wells.md` are real texts, each set under an essay's head that says what it adds.
No scene in them is invented. `showcase.org` is no such text: it says what imprimeur does, for the
README's first picture.

| File | Holds | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `robida.org`, with `journal-telephonoscopique.jpg`, `plaque.webm` and `plaque.png` | Albert Robida, *Le Vingtième Siècle*, Georges Decaux, 1883, first part, chapters V and VI: the telephonoscope | [fr.wikisource](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_Vingti%C3%A8me_Si%C3%A8cle), revisions 12826769 and 2629867 of those chapters. The engraving is Robida's, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Robida_telephonoscope.jpg), public domain |
| `wells.md`, with `lanos-general-intelligence.jpg`, `babble.webm` and `babble.png` | H. G. Wells, *When the Sleeper Wakes*, 1899: the Babble Machines and the General Intelligence Machine | [Project Gutenberg, eBook 775](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/775). The plate is Henri Lanos's for the edition of 1899, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:When_the_Sleeper_Wakes), public domain |
| `showcase.org`, with [`printer-1568.png`](../../docs/images/printer-1568.png) | What imprimeur does, in English | The text is the repository's, under its licence. The woodcut is Jost Amman's of 1568, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Printer_in_1568-ce.png), public domain |

Everything else in the first two says that it is the essay's, by a comment in its source and by
its own words on the page: the table, the code, the diagrams, the notes and the two films, which were
made from the engraving and the plate, and which are under the repository's licence. The sample of
Wells leaves out, and says so, a passage on the novel's colonial police, in the language of its day.

## The check of the quotations

Each of the first two promises that its author's paragraphs are quoted to the letter. A program
checked each paragraph outside the essay's marks against the witnesses above on 2026-11-03: 45 paragraphs and the
note in French, and 10 in English, all found, with one misprint of the French corrected, "de, Wagner",
which the sample says. The two chapters' sources are not copied here. Each dash of dialogue in the
French is written U+2015, the horizontal bar, as this repository holds no em dash; that is the one
sign that differs from the witness.
