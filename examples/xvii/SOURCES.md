# Sources of the samples

| File | Holds | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `descartes.org`, with `planche-balle.svg` and `.webm` | Extracts of the first, second and fourth parts of the *Discours*; a fable; then the sample's own chapters, which show every construct and each kind of opener | René Descartes, 1637, in Victor Cousin's edition, as [Project Gutenberg, ebook 13846](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/13846.txt.utf-8) gives it. Jean de La Fontaine, *Fables*, 1668, as [ebook 56327](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/56327.txt.utf-8) gives it |
| `milton.md`, with `plate-sort.svg` | The opening of *Areopagitica* and a later passage; then the sample's own chapter | John Milton, 1644; [Project Gutenberg, ebook 608](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/608.txt.utf-8), which modernises his spelling |

Everything else in each file says that it is the sample's own: the notices, the headings, the
manchettes, the linking sentences, the table, the programs, the lexicon, the last four
chapters of `descartes.org`, and the drawings, which are under the repository's licence and
copy no printer's ornament. `descartes.org` corrects two misprints in the precepts: *faire*
for *taire*, *examinerois* for *examinerais*. Neither prose was compared with a first edition.
