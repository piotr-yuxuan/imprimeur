## The maqama

The text was to be de Sacy's edition of 1847, *Les séances de Hariri*, with its Arabic commentary in
the margin, checked against Wikisource. That edition could not be read here: HathiTrust, which holds
it in full view (record 102607244, `uc1.c038519685`), answers every fetch with a challenge for robots, and
the Internet Archive's scans of the edition of 1853 and of the first of 1822 have no usable Arabic text.
So the text is Wikisource's, which names no edition, and the glosses come from another source under the
same terms:

- **The notes** are sentences from *Mukhtar al-Sihah*, مختار الصحاح, al-Razi's abridgement of al-Jawhari's
  *Sihah*, which de Sacy's commentary itself quotes, from Arabic Wikisource's
  [مختار الصحاح](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/مختار_الصحاح): the chapters of غ (revision 135940), ت
  (135924), ط (135937), ب (256935), ج (135926), م (135945) and س (286223). Each note quotes one entry, named
  after it, as it stands, without the conjunction that opens it.
- **What the editor changed:** the wiki's own markup is gone; each hemistich of a verse stands on its own
  line, where the wiki separates them by `***`; three headings are added, each taken from the text's own
  words; the narrator's formulas are in red, as a rubric, and Abu Zayd's name in gold.
- **What the editor wrote:** the colophon, تمّت المقامة الصنعانية، وهي الأولى من مقامات الحريري، منقولةً عن ويكي
  مصدر, and the seal, إمبريمور, the name of imprimeur in Arabic letters. The date is the day the sample was
  made.

## The Golestan

- **The notes** are Foroughi's, as Wikisource gives them: a variant of a manuscript, after its sign. پا is
  the manuscript of the Bibliothèque nationale in Paris, copied in 768 AH; س an old manuscript of the
  royal library.
- **What the editor changed:** the wiki's own markup is gone; the chapter's title is one heading, and
  each story's heading names its number, as the wiki's anchors do; each hemistich stands on its own line.
- **What the editor wrote:** the colophon, تمام شد دو حکایت نخست از باب اول گلستان سعدی، به تصحیح
  محمدعلی فروغی، برگرفته از ویکی‌نبشته, and the seal, امپریمور. The date is the day the sample was made.
